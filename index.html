How a player is graded

1. Base points. Per-game stats are converted with your scoring:

2. This week. The model adjusts base points, then blends in the sites:

Model = Base × Matchup × Trend × Vegas × Weather × Health Matchup = 1 + weight × (oppRank − 16.5) / 15.5 Vegas = 1 + weight × (team implied pts − team's usual pts) / team's usual pts Weather = wind 15+ mph: passing −5%, kickers −10% (20+ mph: −10% / −18%) Health = injury status, adjusted by Wed–Fri practice (DNP / limited / full) Projection = (1 − w) × Model + w × average(ESPN, Sleeper, FantasyPros) On bye, Out, or IR → 0

For real players: Base is season points per game from Sleeper. Matchup rank is how many fantasy points the opponent has allowed to that position per game (weeks 1–4; 32 = most allowed). Trend compares snap share over the last two games to the season (for QBs, recent points). Health is the current injury designation. Schedule averages the matchup ranks of the remaining opponents. Start / Flex / Sit calls assume a 12-team league.

3. Floor and ceiling. Half from the position's typical swing (QB ±22%, RB ±30%, WR ±35%, TE ±38%, K ±35%, DST ±45%) and half from the player's own week-to-week swings once he has 3+ games. Two players with close projections? The narrower range is the safer start.

4. Rest of season.

Pts/game = Base × Schedule × Trend × Health Games left = 18 − week (minus bye if still ahead) Total = Pts/game × Games left

Kickers and defenses use the average fantasy points you enter, since PPR doesn't change them.

Data sources

Stats that predict fantasy points

Research on fantasy scoring keeps finding the same thing: opportunity predicts points better than efficiency. Volume stats are stable from week to week, while yards per carry and TD rates swing a lot in small samples. Player pages rank every stat below against the rest of the position.

On every player page

  • Snap share – playing time, the base of everything else.
  • Target share – the strongest single predictor for WRs and TEs.
  • Yards per team pass attempt and yards per snap – free stand-ins for yards per route run.
  • Air yards share (completed) – downfield role.
  • Red-zone share (carries and targets) – touchdown opportunity, steadier than TD totals.
  • Carry share and opportunities per game for RBs.
  • Share of fantasy points from rushing and team carry share for QBs.
  • First downs per target, yards after contact, broken tackles, yards after catch, drop rate.

Added from more sources

  • Expected fantasy points and points over expected – what usage is worth, from play-by-play.
  • WOPR, full air yards share, aDOT, RACR and EPA – nflverse.
  • Separation, YAC over expected, rush yards over expected, stacked boxes, time to throw – NFL Next Gen Stats.
  • First-read targets, catchable and contested targets, interception-worthy throws, play action – FTN charting.
  • End-zone, deep and goal-line shares – play-by-play.
  • Vegas implied totals, weather, practice reports and expert consensus – used in this week's projections.

Still behind paywalls

  • Yards and targets per route run and route participation need route counts from PFF or Fantasy Points Data.
